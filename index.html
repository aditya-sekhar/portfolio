PBI — Payment Behavior & Insights

V1 shipped · in production use

"Proposed, prototyped and now lead an in-house payment risk platform that scores every Comcast transaction in real time, built to displace $6–7M/year of external vendor spend."

It started as a lab-week proof of concept, after noticing what an external fraud and risk vendor was costing us. The demo earned a pitch to the VP of Engineering and business leadership, who greenlit it — I then built the real system alone over roughly two months, before any team existed. It's now a funded product I lead as architect, technical lead and product owner across a 4-person team, and I've personally authored roughly 90% of the codebase to date. The work protects Comcast's multi-billion-dollar payment flows from external fraud actors.

Every transaction is scored in real time by combining offline-trained anomaly-detection and classification models with a runtime rule engine — models offline, decisions online, so the business can retune its risk appetite without a model retrain or a redeploy. A business-facing UI lets risk owners review accounts and manage configuration themselves. V1 is approved for launch and already runs in production batch-replay, generating the reports the business relies on today to act on high-risk accounts, ahead of full payment-gateway integration.

Xerxes — Cross-Partner Identity Platform

Production · 2017–2021

"Made the build-vs-buy case to replace a licensed OpenAM deployment with a stateless, in-house Spring Security identity platform — because per-seat licensing couldn't survive the jump from enterprise users to 30 million subscribers."

Comcast syndicates applications to partner operators — Cox, Shaw, Videotron and others — whose users authenticate against their own identity providers but need Comcast-specific roles inside Comcast applications. The inherited solution was OpenAM: stateful, a black box, and priced per seat at roughly $200K/yr for an enterprise-scale user base that was about to become a 30-million-subscriber one. I made the case to rebuild it stateless on Spring Security, brokering login against SAML or OIDC providers and issuing JWT access and refresh tokens — eliminating the licence and, more importantly, an architecture that could not scale at any price.

Delegated authorization let client teams define their own roles and rights, embedded in the id_token, so a user arriving from a partner's IdP landed in a Comcast application with exactly the right entitlements — without Comcast ever holding those users' credentials. Encryption in transit and at rest, regular security audits and multi-region deployment came with the territory. When a federated provider changed its signature handling and broke the integration in production, I ran the fix.

Approach

One principle, tested four ways

Keep the probabilistic, expensive component off the critical path, and let the deterministic layer do as much of the work as it possibly can. It's the decision at the centre of PBI, and I've kept testing it on my own time — because in fraud and payments, "the model was confident" is not an acceptable answer.

In production — PBI

Models offline, decisions online

Trained models feed a runtime rule engine rather than deciding directly. The business can move its risk appetite without a retrain or a deploy, and every decision stays explainable to an analyst.

Side project — VisionX

Deterministic arithmetic, probabilistic identification

The model reads an engineering drawing and identifies the operations; a separate admin-editable catalogue prices them. The system is structurally unable to hallucinate a cost.

Side project — Trio

A zero-token happy path

An on-device matcher resolves the common case entirely in the browser, for free and offline. A model is only reached for the remainder, so the expensive path is the exception rather than the default.

Advisory

Rule gates before model calls

Advising a friend's startup on an agentic platform, the highest-leverage change was ordering: hard deterministic filters run first, so tokens are only ever spent on what already passed the cheap test.
